# Aztec Code

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `aztec` — also `aztec-code`, `azteccode`
- **Accepts:** any byte string, up to roughly 3000 characters of text or 1900 bytes of binary
- **Payload:** `BOARDING-4471`
- **Modules:** 15 × 15
