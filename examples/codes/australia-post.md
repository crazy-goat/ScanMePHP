# Australia Post

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `australia-post` — also `auspost`, `australia-post-4state`, `customer-barcode`
- **Accepts:** an 8-digit sorting code, optionally followed by 5, 8, 10 or 15 characters of customer information — 8 and 15 digits only
- **Payload:** `96130590AB CD`
- **Modules:** 103 × 3
