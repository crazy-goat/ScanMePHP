# GS1 DataBar Limited

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `databar-limited` — also `gs1-databar-limited`, `rss-limited`, `rss-ltd`
- **Accepts:** 13 digits starting 0 or 1, or 14 with a correct check digit, optionally prefixed (01)
- **Payload:** `01234567890128`
- **Modules:** 74 × 1
