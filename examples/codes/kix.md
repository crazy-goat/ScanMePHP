# KIX

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `kix` — also `kix-code`, `klantindex`, `postnl`
- **Accepts:** 1 to 18 digits and capital letters, typically a postcode, house number and additions
- **Payload:** `2500GG30250`
- **Modules:** 87 × 3
