# PDF417

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `pdf417` — also `pdf-417`
- **Accepts:** any byte string, up to roughly 1850 characters of text or 1100 bytes of binary
- **Payload:** `SHIP TO: 123 Main St.`
- **Modules:** 171 × 4
- **Error correction:** 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
