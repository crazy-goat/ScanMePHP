# Micro QR Code

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `micro-qr` — also `microqr`, `micro-qrcode`
- **Accepts:** up to 35 digits, 21 alphanumeric characters or 15 bytes, depending on the version and error correction level
- **Payload:** `LOT4471`
- **Modules:** 13 × 13
- **Error correction:** L, M, Q
