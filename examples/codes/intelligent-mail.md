# Intelligent Mail

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `intelligent-mail` — also `imb`, `usps-imb`, `onecode`, `usps4cb`
- **Accepts:** 20 digits of tracking code, then 0, 5, 9 or 11 digits of routing code
- **Payload:** `01234567094987654321-01234`
- **Modules:** 129 × 3
