# ITF

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `itf` — also `interleaved-2-of-5`, `i25`
- **Accepts:** an even number of digits, or an odd number with the check digit option
- **Payload:** `1234567890`
- **Modules:** 99 × 1
