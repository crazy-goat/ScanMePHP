# RM4SCC

[Back to the index](../index.md)

- **Name:** `rm4scc` — also `royal-mail`, `royal-mail-4state`, `rm4scc-cbc`
- **Accepts:** 1 to 50 digits and capital letters, typically a postcode and delivery point suffix
- **Payload:** `LE28HS`
- **Modules:** 59 × 3
